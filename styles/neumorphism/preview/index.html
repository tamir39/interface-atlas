A little room to read

There is a shelf beside the window where the small things gather: a smooth stone, a folded map, a cup with a chipped blue rim. None of them asks for attention. Together, they make a place feel familiar.

On a quiet morning, the light reaches the shelf before it reaches the table. It moves slowly across the wood. A page turns. Outside, someone stops to fasten a loose shoelace, then carries on.

Reading can be like that: a small pause with enough room around it. A line ends, the eye returns, and the next thought is waiting. There is no need to hurry past the space between.

The material, with boundaries

One surface.
Two directions of light.

A raised choice catches light above and shade below. The selected choice turns that relief inward. Words and controls sit on an opaque, unshaded center.

What does the style change?

The workbench plane, soft shells, and inner faces share the same opaque theme Surface color. Opposing shadows create shallow relief around a 12px rim. Selected draft width turns the relief inward, with a check, Selected text, pressed semantics, and an Ink outline.

Soft shells keep the theme’s card corners. Each inner corner is the corresponding outer radius minus 12px, clamped at zero. Ordinary fields and actions retain the theme’s control geometry and separate Raised or Primary fills.

What comes from the theme?

Palette, declared font stacks, and base geometry come from each Atlas theme’s actual preview tokens. This is one limited specimen across nine identities; it does not load nine complete applications.

Editorial’s reading palette and type suit this quiet task. Paired inset relief is an explicit departure from its ordinary selective elevation.

What is applied, and what is saved?

The width buttons and form edit a draft. Apply reading settings validates the entire draft and updates this sample’s title, text size, line spacing, and maximum line width. Editing leaves the last applied sample visible. Reset example restores both draft and sample.

Nothing is saved or sent. Reload restores the example. These controls do not change browser, operating-system, or other reading preferences.

What happens with device preferences?

Press feedback starts Off. On adds only 1px of downward press travel with a 120ms transform transition. Reduced motion removes that travel and transition. Shadows change instantly. Forced colors removes shadows while preserving explicit boundaries, selection, errors, and focus. System appearance follows live device changes.

Where did Neumorphism come from?

Jason Kelley is credited with suggesting the name; Michał Malewicz documented and popularized the direction. Oleksandr Plyuto created the influential banking concept. These are distinct contributions, not a sole-inventor claim.

The layout, reading passage, and interaction code are original Atlas work. The source notes separate historical references from copied material; no third-party component code or assets are imported.
