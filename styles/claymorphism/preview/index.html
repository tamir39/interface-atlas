The material, with boundaries

A soft perimeter.
A clear center.

Domed edges suggest volume. An opaque inner surface keeps every label, input, and message on a stable color.

What does the style change?

Selected clay shells explicitly use a 28px outer radius, 12px padding, and a 16px opaque inner radius. Their paired outer and inset shadows stay around the readable center. Ordinary fields and the work panel keep the chosen theme’s control and card geometry. The style does not preserve every corner.

What comes from the theme?

Palette, semantic color pairs, and declared font stacks come from each Atlas theme’s actual preview tokens. This is a shared, limited specimen, not the theme’s full layout.

Play’s expressive identity has affinity with this small creative task. Clay explicitly replaces its hard depth and shell geometry.

Declared body stack: 'Manrope', 'Segoe UI', Arial, sans-serif

What happens with device preferences?

Press feedback starts Off. On adds only 1px of downward press travel with a 120ms transform transition. Reduced motion removes that travel and transition. Forced colors removes shadows and decorative artwork, using system colors while retaining control boundaries, selection text, errors, and focus. System appearance follows live device changes.

Where did Claymorphism come from?

Michał Malewicz named and popularized Claymorphism in 2021. Inflated forms and soft dimensional illustration have broader precedents. This layout, CSS artwork, and interaction code are original Atlas work. The source notes distinguish historical references and implementation references from copied material.
