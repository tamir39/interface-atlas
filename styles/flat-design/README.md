# Flat Design

Opaque color planes, typography, spacing and explicit control states organize a two-dimensional interface. This **form** entry accompanies a theme. Rounded shapes, substantial content and working application controls can remain; removing depth does not require removing useful information.

**Atlas entry:** original interpretation by Tamir (Phi Vuong Tuong Tam), with Codex AI assistance. **Kate Moran / Nielsen Norman Group** are credited for published analysis and signifier research; **Microsoft**, including Moneta Ho Kushner's authored Windows account, for an influential design precedent; **Designmodo** for its separate Flat UI kit. No sole inventor is asserted. [Sources and credits](sources.md) distinguish those roles and artifact-specific terms; no source code, artwork, fonts or article prose is imported.

## Fit and limits

| Candidate use | Atlas recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Consumer or service interface | Consider it when solid controls, persistent labels and explicit feedback support familiar tasks. |
| Product dashboard | Assess grouping, density and state comprehension; removing elevation may require clearer spacing or rules. |
| Selected product surfaces | Apply a defined treatment to actual components; preserve the project's content and workflows. |
| Material or expressive identity | Removing shadows, relief or translucency changes that identity. Record the departure and assess the affected surface. |

These are conditional Atlas judgments. Flatness guarantees neither usability, accessibility nor performance. **Minimalism** concerns broader restraint in content and features. [Web Brutalism](../web-brutalism/README.md) has a separate content-first composition; [Neobrutalism](../neobrutalism/README.md) adds outlined forms and hard offset depth. Material systems have their own authorship and elevation models.
