Study 02 / hard edgesTheme identity supplies color, type, and geometry. The style adds emphatic structure.

The rule behind the treatment

Big character.
Clear decisions.

Editorial: an intentional tension with its quieter reading rhythm. Keep the heavy treatment to short, focused surfaces.

What comes from the theme?

The nine profiles supply their actual preview palette, declared local font stacks, and corner geometry. This is a selected profile applied to one shared specimen, with explicit style overrides. It does not load a complete theme or prove full theme compatibility. Read the selected theme reference before applying it to a project.

What makes this Neobrutalism?

Opaque color blocks, 2px foreground outlines, 4px hard shadows with no blur, and direct typographic hierarchy. The style emphasizes structure without making every surface compete. Detailed reading and form content keep solid, stable backgrounds.

How does press feedback work?

Off is the default. On optionally moves a pressed card or button 2px and reduces its shadow from 4px to 2px. Reduced motion disables that movement. Selected labels, borders, and semantic pressed states remain immediate in every setting, including forced colors.

Who is credited?

This is an original Atlas specimen. Samuel Breznjak’s Neobrutalism components (ekmas) is credited as an implementation reference, not as the inventor of the style. See source notes and reuse boundaries, including the selected themes’ provenance.
