# Evaluate design references

Status: adaptation draft, 2026-10-20. Original Atlas synthesis by **Tamir (Phi Vuong Tuong Tam), with Codex AI assistance**. No product validation is claimed.

Read for a chosen external reference or an unresolved visual-direction decision. Existing Atlas themes and resolved project rules do not require another source survey. Carry accepted decisions into the project's self-contained design document.

## Evaluate the selected contribution

Name the decision and the source contribution: grouping, a type relationship, component behavior or a visual technique. Inspect the relevant material and enough context to interpret it. Reuse the existing provenance record for creator, URL, inspected revision/date and reuse terms; do not create a second ledger or reread content solely to fingerprint it.

Trace the proposed rule through the source's description, tokens and examples. Check contradictions, omitted consequential states and unsupported claims such as “accessible” or “production ready.” A dependency's notice does not establish the reuse terms of the source's prose, fonts, imagery or code. Keep unresolved artifacts as reference links rather than bundling them.

Retain, adapt or reject the idea against the approved project contract and chosen theme. State the reason for an exception. Resolve accepted ideas into actual project values, content order, applicable states and behavior. Source preferences do not silently override user choices or capability boundaries. Identify performed checks and pending questions at their actual evidence level.

## Compare candidates on equivalent work

Use a comparison only when theme fit remains consequentially unresolved. Preserve an explicit user selection. Start with candidate READMEs; inspect detailed rules only for plausible candidates. Two can be sufficient.

Choose a representative task and surface. Hold decision content, labels, data, available actions and meaningful states equivalent. Include relevant content pressure, such as a long title, missing image or validation message. Use original or owner-provided content with known rights; better imagery or shorter copy must not give one candidate an artificial advantage.

Let hierarchy, typography and composition express each theme. Compare action recognition, information grouping, readability, state interpretation, adaptation needs and supported contexts. Record task, constants, differences, tradeoffs, evidence type and selection or uncertainty. A document sketch is desk reasoning; rendered checks and observations of users are separate evidence. Do not invent a numerical usability score. When task differences are immaterial, use the brief and owner preference to resolve the aesthetic choice.
