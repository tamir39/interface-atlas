## Project proposals and missing inputs

Proposals include Layered rather than Botanical/Play; a four-stage full-page flow; 760px maximum main width and task-sized headings; the exact field set, Unicode-count bounds and workshop range 1–30; custom feedback ownership; canonical in-memory draft plus contextual review return; no optional effects; bundled version-matched translations; memory-only unsent answers by default; a 15-second local waiting threshold; no automatic effect retry; and a dispatch gate requiring an approved recovery identity/discovery contract. These are design decisions, not established service capabilities or measured improvements.

Missing inputs remain assigned product/language/service/privacy owners; approved field/schema/normalization/email policy; real service descriptions and follow-up copy; translation packs and Vietnamese review; contact-use/retention policy; font delivery package; authoritative receipt/deduplication/lookup/expiry contract; and verified retention/discovery mechanisms. Public personal-data collection and dispatch remain gated on the relevant contracts. No authentication, endpoint, support route, durable save or status service was fabricated.
