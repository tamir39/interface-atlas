## Actionable finding

### Medium — reconcile late successful observations without losing a confirmed receipt

**Location:** `examples/skill-evaluation/bilingual-service/first-draft.md`, **Submission, uncertain outcomes and contract gates**, particularly the service-contract list and the paragraph beginning “Matching authoritative receipt allows”; **Validation and open inputs**, the future delayed/out-of-order-receipt tests.

**Trigger and consequence:** A status observation for submitted action A is delayed; a newer authorized response confirms receipt and displays its real reference. The older same-action observation then arrives with pending state, missing history or contradictory rejection. Identity matching alone does not establish which response governs. The design prevents a *failed* status lookup from erasing a receipt but does not define how a late *successful* observation is ordered, rejected or shown alongside confirmed evidence. An implementation could regress the page to uncertainty, hide the receipt/reference, or enable correction/retry after an obsolete rejection, undermining the one-request flow.

**Requirement and evidence:** The brief requires truthful uncertain-outcome recovery and a distinction between a retained draft, accepted request and completed service. This is an omitted document decision, not an observed failure or a theme issue. Atlas `knowledge/uncertain-outcomes.md`, **Cancellation and stale evidence** and **Safe retry and recovery**, call for action/payload correlation and documented freshness/ordering, retention of confirmed history, and explicit treatment of contradictions that cannot be ordered.

**Counterevidence considered:** The design correctly separates action and attempt identities, freezes submitted answers, gates dispatch on recovery identity/discovery, requires lookup by the original action ID if later mapping fails, blocks overlapping submissions, keeps missing history unknown, and says a failed lookup cannot erase a confirmed receipt. Its service gates also require authoritative states and consistency-lag semantics. None of those states which same-action successful observation may supersede another, or what the interface does when two authorized observations disagree without a reliable order. Merely listing an out-of-order-receipt test later does not supply that rule. The existing unresolved-contract gate appropriately prevents public dispatch; this finding is a requirement to close that contract/design gap before enabling it.

**Bounded correction:** Require the service owner to supply reliable observation ordering/version semantics or explicitly state that ordering is unavailable. Define a local reconciliation rule that preserves the known receipt/reference and immutable action history; ignores provably older evidence for current-state replacement; and displays an unresolved inconsistency with supported status/reconciliation controls when observations cannot be ordered. Keep the existing no-overlap/retry gate while contradictory evidence is unresolved. Do not choose response arrival order, client clocks, or an invented service monotonicity guarantee.
