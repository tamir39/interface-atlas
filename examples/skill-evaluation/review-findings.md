## Opt-in review evidence

- **R01–R02:** ordinary implementation and a narrow typo probe opened neither Atlas skill. Explicit review opened the review entry, produced reports and changed no input. This tests fresh-context explicit-path behavior and supplied discovery metadata; it does not test a host's automatic registry or installation.
- **R03–R05:** the controlled fixture's initial reviewer found both seeded P1 conflicts (Confirm executes a different version; Cancel claims a stop before evidence) plus a supported P2 missing Reject/Edit path. Findings name consequences, locations, counterevidence, bounded corrections and unavailable runtime evidence.
- **R06:** final known-case review reuses shared references in one grouped context; operations is separate. No catalog or detailed theme reread occurred without a concrete inheritance question. Byte totals include supplied project documents and are reported separately from Atlas guidance.
- **R07:** the first grouped review found no actionable issue and missed IR01–IR03. Root observations were frozen before that comparison. A conditional transition profile was then added, frozen and given to a fresh reviewer without expected answers or old findings. It independently reported the same three material omissions. Initial reports are retained, not replaced. Operations independently found IR04; root accepted it. No unsupported finding was established in these inspected reports. This small qualitative agreement does not estimate recall/precision or prove causal effectiveness.

The controlled positive fixture used the initial review version; the final review version was exercised on the real trial drafts. Selected P0 sections contain embedded historical comparisons; reviewers/generators did not follow their example links or use them as current product observations. Familiarity and shared guidance limit independence, especially for the three known briefs.

The final preparation-entry rerun chose **Botanical**, not the initial run's Layered. It returned 119,804 content bytes (117,264 Atlas-only) across 52 content reads, plus 16 metadata-only operations. It preserves the same service brief and major task/capability boundaries. C03/C07 and the local language transition remained partial in its first draft (IR06/IR07), corrected after review; remaining service behavior is still open/gated. This different theme/selection and fresh context prevent isolating the hash-guidance change as the sole cause of the reading difference. Final review did not miss the root ordering concern: it explicitly addressed it as an already gated input, a justified classification difference.
