# Pocket Practice design

> Reviewed example: originally prepared with the initial updated preparation skill, then explicitly reviewed and corrected at document level on 2026-10-17. The immutable first draft and correction evidence accompany this example. No runtime, service, accessibility or usability result is implied.

Status: project proposal, 2026-10-17; document preparation only. Theme: Layered 0.1.0-draft, `atlas-layered-research-2026-10-15`. No interface, storage implementation, or user research was supplied or tested.

## Requirements, assumptions and direction

The supplied brief defines a responsive personal practice web app for independent learners: browse/filter text lessons, bookmark, plan weekly practice, finish with a short note, edit a goal, and revisit actual completions. English interface text may surround Vietnamese lessons. Provide Light, Dark and System appearances. Records and preferences survive reload in the same browser when storage works. Accounts, synchronization, backend, AI and agents are outside the product. There is no language toggle.

Everything below about navigation, markup, data shapes, save boundaries, conflicts, removal, media and recovery is a **project proposal**, pending the contracts named below. Lesson content, brand assets, font binaries, stack and final data model are missing. The brief supplies requirements, not observed learner behavior. Our hypothesis is that a small weekly plan and visible saved-state wording help learners resume; validate that with intended users rather than inventing personas, streaks or achievement claims.

Select **Layered** for comfortable controls, quiet opaque regions and a coherent rounded vocabulary. Botanical offers calm reading but its expressive serif identity needs more application adaptation. Play supports learning but its hard shadows and colorful emphasis are less suited to this brief's repeated calm practice. Retain Layered's roles and geometry; reduce its large introductory display, use text-first lesson rows, and replace its disposable specimen state with the local contracts here. No optional effects or animation are selected; feedback is immediate.

## Tasks, information and content

Use four labeled destinations: **Lessons**, **This week**, **History**, **Settings**. Lessons is the default. Primary navigation uses ordinary links and a visible current marker with `aria-current="page"`; filters are controls, not tabs. Each logical page has one h1, an updated document title and a main landmark. The header contains the product name; skip navigation leads to main.

Lessons presents title, a one-sentence introduction, labeled **Search lessons**, **Topic** (All topics default), **Bookmarked only**, result count, then a fixed-order lesson list. Each row has title link, summary, topic, supplied estimated duration if available, and a separately labeled **Bookmark [title]** toggle. Never nest buttons in links. Search matches title and summary case-insensitively; retain Vietnamese diacritics and document any later accent-insensitive adaptation. Changing filters preserves bookmarks and current practice. **Clear filters** resets all three and focuses Search. Zero matches says “No lessons match these filters”; no supplied catalog says “Lessons aren't available yet.” Neither implies missing personal records.

Lesson detail orders title, summary/metadata, full lesson text, then **Start practice** and **Plan practice**. Bookmark is secondary. Render content as text or an explicitly sanitized, owner-approved subset; user notes are always plain text. English page language is `en`; mark Vietnamese passages `lang="vi"`. No fabricated duration, translations, imagery, audio or video. A content owner must deliver stable lesson IDs, topics, ordering, accessible lesson structure and content rights before catalog release.

This week orders the dated week heading, current goal and **Edit goal**, seven dated day groups, then **Edit weekly plan**. Proposal: Monday–Sunday, local calendar dates, one optional lesson per day; no drag interaction or calendar service. History orders newest actual completion date first, then lesson title and saved note; opening a completion shows the historical lesson-title snapshot, date and note, with **Open current lesson** only if its ID still resolves. Never turn a planned slot, bookmark, opened lesson or recovered unfinished practice into completed work.

Empty copy offers the relevant next action: bookmarks → “Bookmark a lesson to find it here”; week → “Choose a lesson for your week” / **Browse lessons**; history → “Your completed practices will appear here” / **Browse lessons**. Settings groups Appearance and Browser storage. No sample history is mixed with real records.

## Type, geometry and adaptation

Use local `"Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif` initially. Layered's proposed Plus Jakarta Sans remains an optional packaging decision, with normal 400/500/600/700 only after Vietnamese glyph and actual-rendering checks. No remote font request or claimed font availability. Project type proposal: h1 2rem/1.25, h2 1.5rem/1.3, lesson title 1.25rem/1.35 at 600; body 1rem/1.6 at 400; controls at least 1rem/1.5 at 500–600; metadata .875rem/1.5 at 500. Normal tracking; no uppercase headings. Readable paragraphs max 65ch. Dates can use tabular numerals; notes and names wrap without clipping, including long Vietnamese diacritics and unbroken input.

Retain spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px: labels 8px from fields, related controls 12px apart, groups 24–32px apart. Panels use 24px radius, controls 12px, optional short navigation pills 999px; modal 28px. No resting shadows or decorative cards around every row. Controls and whole checkbox labels provide at least 44×44px targets.

Phone proposal: at 320px and above use 16px gutters, single-column content, static wrapping top navigation, stacked filters and day groups, 16px panel inset. At 640px, use 24px gutters/insets and allow filters side by side only while labels fit. At 960px, use a 224px Low navigation column and remaining Surface work region, overall max 1200px; reading remains 65ch. A week remains readable dated groups rather than seven compressed desktop columns. The DOM order remains navigation, page heading, goal, work, supporting actions. These breakpoints are provisional content-fit decisions, to revise after rendering.

No fixed bottom bar, hover-only information or horizontal page scrolling. At zoom or enlarged text, reflow to the narrow composition; controls/errors wrap. Editors use min(100%-32px, 560px), max-height fitting the viewport and a scrollable interior with actions in normal flow. The onscreen keyboard must not cover the focused field or Save. Native semantic lists, buttons, associated fields, fieldsets and links are proposed; no custom grid keyboard model is needed.

## Local state and acknowledgement contract

Proposal: use transactional IndexedDB, independent of any framework; browser support remains a release gate. The validated local record owns bookmarks, dated plans, goal, completions and appearance. Packaged lessons own catalog content. URLs own destination, lesson ID, week and filters; Back restores those plus prior scroll/focus where available. Editing drafts have separate records keyed by editor/session ID; a small status layer owns pending/error state. Derived counts come only from validated records.

Proposed envelope includes schemaVersion, non-reused generation, revision and stable object IDs. Completions include unique practice-session ID, lesson ID/title snapshot, note, completion instant, local date/timezone at completion and optional plan-occurrence ID. Preserve historical dates after timezone changes. A draft stores its base generation/object revision, edited values and draft sequence. This is a contract proposal, not an implemented schema. Engineering must finalize serialization, Unicode length units, supported-browser baseline, shared record/draft capacity budget and migration policy before writable release. Do not enable unbounded accumulation or silently evict history. A capacity rejection preserves the draft and offers a text copy.

Goals/plans/completions use explicit **Save goal**, **Save plan**, **Finish practice**. Notes/goals/plans autosave only a recoverable draft after 500ms idle; distinguish “Draft saved in this browser” from applied changes. Serialize/coalesce draft writes; each completion acknowledges only its captured sequence. Newer text remains unsaved until its own acknowledgement. On reload offer **Resume draft** or **Discard draft** with lesson/week/base context; no expiry is proposed. A removed or changed base requires review.

Each explicit save freezes its submitted controls briefly, preserves readable values and serializes writes; guarded focusable `aria-disabled` prevents duplicate activation through every entry point. Status says “Saving in this browser…” and only transaction completion allows “Saved in this browser”. Failures retain the editor and latest draft, show “Not saved. Keep this page open and try again” plus **Retry** and **Download text copy**. A failed storage operation does not mark correct input invalid. If draft autosave also failed, explain that reload/tab closure can lose the current text. Never claim synchronization, permanent retention or a verified file download.

All mutations check generation and the relevant base revision inside the same read/write transaction. Revision checks outside the write transaction are insufficient. Bookmark desired-state operations may apply to the latest generation without replaying a whole snapshot. Goal/plan edits with changed bases stop: show saved and proposed values, offer **Use saved version** or **Review my changes**. For plans review only the days the learner changed; reapply those day values after a new transactional base check, preserving other days. A further conflict returns to review. Goal replacement similarly checks the displayed saved goal revision. No automatic overwrite option.

## End-to-end editing, practice and recovery

**Bookmark:** activate the labeled pressed-state button; show pending beside that lesson, retain the last acknowledged state until commit, then change the marker and saved count. Failure offers Retry without a false bookmark. Removing uses the same inverse toggle; no time-limited toast Undo is needed.

**Plan/goal:** Plan practice opens a date choice for the displayed week; Edit weekly plan exposes seven labeled day selects with “No lesson”. Goal proposal: required plain text 1–160 Unicode scalar values; optional weekly target, integer 1–7 sessions. A completion note is required plain text 1–280 scalar values. Trim outer whitespace; retain internal whitespace/diacritics. Show constraints/counts, but validate errors on explicit submission. A custom validation controller suppresses native interactive validation for these forms, collects all failures including Enter submission, associates text via `aria-describedby`/`aria-invalid`, and focuses a linked summary. Correction clears its stale error. Final limits are product proposals pending model approval.

Editors use a named native dialog with initial field focus, trapped Tab through native modality, visible Cancel and stable opener return. Clean Cancel/Escape closes. Dirty Cancel/Escape/close requests switch the same dialog to **Keep editing** / **Discard changes**, focusing Keep editing; Escape there returns to editing. Backdrop does not dismiss. Confirmed discard removes the durable draft before claiming it is gone; deletion failure retains it and offers Retry. Pending commits cannot be dismissed; announce the brief wait, then follow the acknowledged result. Forced closure retains the last acknowledged draft. Missing modal support uses a full-page editor with the same contract.

**Practice:** Start creates a stable session/draft before presenting lesson plus note and Finish. Reopening resumes that unfinished session. On Finish, validate, then atomically insert one completion for that session and remove its draft. Repeat submission recognizes the existing completion; it cannot add a duplicate. A planned occurrence becomes completed only through that record, in the same transaction; retry preserves identity. Successful Finish opens its History detail and focuses its heading; failure leaves practice and note intact. Unscheduled practice may complete without a plan. Removing a planned slot uses Save plan and never removes a completion. History deletion/editing is not proposed; correcting historical records needs a separate owner decision before adding controls.

**Draft retirement:** Every editor/session draft has a non-reused identity and an active/retired eligibility record within the same atomic storage boundary as its writes. Discard retires that identity and removes its retained text in one transaction. Every queued, resumed or other-tab draft writer must check active eligibility and the expected draft revision inside its write transaction; a retired or obsolete writer cannot recreate the draft. Serialize work at that boundary: a writer committed before retirement is removed by discard; a writer arriving after retirement is rejected. A successful Finish atomically writes the unique completion, retires that practice draft identity and removes its text. Do not reuse a retired identity when starting another editor/session. A failed discard or Finish leaves the active draft and its recovery context available and makes no success claim. Engineering must verify the proposed atomic scope and retirement retention against the browser/storage contract before enabling durable behavior.

**Interruption:** normal route changes preserve acknowledged drafts and explain how to resume. If the newest draft write failed, in-app exit offers Keep editing, Retry draft save, or Leave and lose the unsaved portion. Browser unload warning is supplementary and cannot guarantee interception, especially on mobile. New routes focus h1 at the top; Back/Forward restore prior state without forcing a new top scroll. Missing lesson/completion IDs offer the nearest list and a clear explanation.

**Startup:** show “Loading saved practice…” until classification. A genuinely absent record offers “No saved practice was found in this browser” and **Start a plan**, never “You've never practised”. Valid data restores it. Denied/blocked/unavailable access offers Retry and explicit temporary mode. Temporary mode uses memory, a persistent “Changes last only while this page stays open” banner and text-copy actions; it never autosaves empty defaults over inaccessible data.

Malformed or unsupported data enters guarded read-only recovery: preserve the original, suspend normal writes, identify the issue without calling every failure corruption, and offer Retry. **Download readable recovery data** appears only when the raw value is actually accessible and is labeled diagnostic, not a restorable backup. Do not silently downgrade newer schemas. No migration is authorized by this draft. Blocked upgrades ask other tabs to close and then retry.

A recovery-only **Start empty** action states exactly which local records/drafts would be discarded, offers available text copies, and requires explicit confirmation. Replacement transaction writes a new non-reused generation and clears the affected records atomically. Every writer/draft save rechecks generation, including older tabs; old operations are rejected and their drafts held separately for review. Failed reset preserves the original and temporary draft. Never delete the database as a shortcut around generation checks.

Bound recovery downloads to plain-text copies of readable goal/plan/notes or the current draft. A download initiation is not proof that a file was retained. No full backup/import/merge facility is proposed. Browser clearing, eviction, profile removal and device loss can remove local data; a second browser-store copy would not solve that boundary.

## Appearance, accessibility and evidence gates

Appearance is a labeled native select with Light, Dark, System (default). Apply a validated saved choice early; System follows live OS changes. Apply a new choice immediately but acknowledge persistence only after commit; failed writes say “Appearance changed for this page only” and offer Retry. Malformed choice falls back to System without overwriting unrelated records. Respect reduced motion through immediate changes; no motion preference UI is needed. Focus is immediate and never waits for status, color or animation; forced colors use system Highlight. Avoid redundant assertive announcements: one polite save-status region, persistent readable errors and deliberate focus for validation/navigation. Bookmark state and completion always have text/semantics as well as color.

Performed: selective frozen-source reading, requirement mapping and document contradiction reconciliation. No code, rendered page, keyboard, screen-reader, service or user checks were performed. Inherited contrast arithmetic is separately labeled above.

Before release, engineering must demonstrate successful reload of records/preferences and drafts; blocked/full/malformed/newer data paths; quota aborts without partial completion; duplicate Finish; failed reset; two-tab conflict and a second conflict during review; stale write after replacement; reload during save; and no history fabrication. Test keyboard and screen-reader flow across filtering, editor errors, dirty exit, storage recovery and History, plus actual English/Vietnamese font rendering, 320px reflow, text enlargement, 400% zoom, onscreen keyboard, forced colors, reduced motion and all appearances. Content/product owners must settle dates, limits, history correction and content rights. User observation must test whether learners can distinguish planned, draft, saved and completed work. These are future evidence, not a verdict.

Additional required implementation trace after document review: Exercise queued autosave after Discard and Finish, including an older tab; verify no retired draft returns and failure preserves the active draft.
