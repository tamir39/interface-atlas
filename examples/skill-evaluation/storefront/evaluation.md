The source/adaptation dependencies were read for credits and terms. Embedded historical comparisons in the selected knowledge chapters are not project observations or adopted verdicts.

New project proposals include the catalog/product/cart structure, URL search/filter contract, grid thresholds, image frame, native variant inputs, quantity/line/payload limits, transactional IndexedDB cart v1, explicit temporary mode/conflict reapplication, immutable quote and checkout snapshots, recovery identity before dispatch and a 10-second local delayed-feedback threshold. They are reversible design decisions or implementation contracts, not existing capabilities. The brief supplies commercial authority ownership; this design proposes the frontend boundaries without selecting a backend, provider or application stack.

## Important open inputs and blocked capabilities

Merchandising/brand must supply real catalog taxonomy, variants, factual product content, purchase limits, product media with rights/crop records and an approved font package. Storage/frontend owners must confirm supported browsers, bounds, atomic transaction support, schema/version/conflict behavior and recovery-identity retention.

Backend/business owners must supply monetary representation and rounding, current price/stock access, limits and quote semantics; taxes/shipping/discount rules, quote validity, changed-price acceptance and itemized authoritative totals remain unresolved. Checkout requires immutable payload/action correlation, duplicate-protection scope and retention, authorized lookup and its consistency/history semantics, terminal/partial evidence, permitted hosted URL/return rules, retry pacing, cancellation scope and a real reconciliation destination. Checkout dispatch remains gated until verified recovery is possible, including if local identity storage fails. No endpoint, payment vendor, reservation timer or successful order is asserted.
