## Actionable finding

### Medium — qualify same-action checkout evidence before it can replace a newer outcome

**Location:** `examples/skill-evaluation/storefront/first-draft.md`, **Authoritative review and checkout handoff**, especially “Matching service evidence may supply a permitted hosted URL” and the following lost-reply/status/hosted-return rules; **External contract gates and owners**, the hosted-checkout and support rows; **Accessibility, motion and validation**, the future service-test list.

**Trigger and consequence:** A checkout/status observation for action A is delayed. A later authorized observation for the same action establishes a completed or otherwise terminal outcome and the purchased cart adjustment is reconciled. The earlier observation then arrives with pending status, a rejection or a still-usable-looking hosted URL. Matching identity and payload alone do not say which evidence is current. The document has no rule for preventing an older same-action success from replacing the known outcome, presenting an obsolete handoff, or changing the availability of another checkout. If observations contradict without reliable ordering, the current text also leaves the displayed outcome and safe next action undefined.

**Requirement and evidence:** The brief requires truthful recovery of unknown checkout outcomes and authoritative commercial facts. This is a document-level state/recovery gap, not an observed service bug. Atlas `knowledge/uncertain-outcomes.md`, **Cancellation and stale evidence** and **Safe retry and recovery**, require identity plus documented ordering/freshness; arrival order and client clocks do not establish authority. Confirmed evidence must remain available when unorderable observations conflict.

**Counterevidence considered:** The draft correctly rejects obsolete quotes for a different cart revision, binds checkout to a reviewed quote/snapshot, separates action identity from editable intent, blocks overlapping dispatch, and requires backend reconciliation before reporting a final order or changing purchased quantities. It also explicitly gates live handoff on unresolved service contracts. These are substantial protections, but quote/cart version matching does not settle late evidence for the same checkout action. The gate requests lookup consistency/history and terminal evidence without specifying the service ordering evidence or local reconciliation rule that the UI must apply. A future test of status lag/expiry does not define the behavior.

**Bounded correction:** Add a service-owned gate for the version/sequence or other authoritative ordering semantics of checkout/status evidence. Define the local reducer behavior: correlate action/payload, retain confirmed history, prevent demonstrably older evidence from regressing the current state, and expose an inconsistency with a supported reconciliation action when observations cannot be ordered. Hold overlapping/new effects under the existing gate until resolved. State how late hosted URLs and purchased-cart adjustments are handled under that same rule. Do not invent a monotonic service status model or use response arrival time as its substitute.

## Relevant open inputs

- Backend/hosted-checkout owner: exact effect boundary, action/payload equality, duplicate-protection scope/retention, authorized lookup and outcome evidence remain launch gates. The current draft correctly leaves handoff disabled until those contracts are supplied and verified; their mere absence is not reported as a defect.
- In that contract, prove that the original retained action identity is sufficient for recovery if the response is lost or the later action-to-operation/session mapping cannot be persisted. Otherwise specify independently authorized discovery and gate dispatch on its availability. The draft's pre-dispatch identity rule is a useful boundary; a persisted identifier alone must not be mistaken for a proven lookup capability.
- Quote/business owner must define authoritative itemization, USD representation/rounding, changed-fact acceptance, validity/expiry, tax/shipping scope and revalidation at handoff. The current quote-binding proposal must be resolved against that contract, rather than assuming stock reservation or final taxes.
- Merchandising/brand and frontend/storage owners still owe the real catalog, media/rights and font package, purchase limits, supported-browser/atomic storage evidence and upgrade policy. No fixtures were treated as real product photography.
