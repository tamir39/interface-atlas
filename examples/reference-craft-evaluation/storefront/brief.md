# Form & Field — trial brief

Fictional project: an independent homeware studio's image-led responsive webstore with roughly 50 products. English interface; display money in USD. The brand should feel calm and design-led while purchase actions stay clear.

Required guest tasks: browse the collection; search and filter; inspect accurate product images; choose an available color/size variant and quantity; add products to a cart; edit quantities and remove lines; review authoritative prices, availability, and totals; hand off to hosted checkout. A cart draft must survive same-browser reload when storage works. The backend will own authoritative prices, stock, quote totals, and checkout. Connection and browser storage can fail; prices and availability can change; unknown checkout outcomes require truthful recovery.

Do not invent working endpoints, reservation timers, successful orders, legal guarantees, or a chosen provider. Propose handoff boundaries and identify unresolved contract gates. No account, wishlist, AI/agent, or loyalty flows are requested. Do not represent Gallery's generated illustration fixtures as real product photographs.
