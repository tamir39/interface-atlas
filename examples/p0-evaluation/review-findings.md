# P0 chapter review findings

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Atlas analysis by **Tamir (Phi Vuong Tuong Tam), with Codex AI assistance**. Three isolated reviewers inspected one primary chapter each and relevant crosslinks; root consolidated duplicate findings, verified source scope and applied corrections before generation. All six unique findings were confirmed addressed by their reviewers. This is document evidence, not an exhaustive correctness or runtime verdict.

| ID | Priority | Gap in the initial draft | Correction in 0.2-draft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K01 | P2 | Completion after continued editing could mark a newer draft clean | [Save snapshots](../../knowledge/state-and-recovery.md#save-snapshots-and-completion) bind acknowledgement to candidate A; later B remains dirty. Ordering, failures and invalidated generations are explicit. [Forms](../../knowledge/forms-and-navigation.md#pending-actions-and-available-controls) carries the same rule. |
| K02 | P2 | Reset/import/delete could be undone accidentally by a delayed autosave or stale tab | [Replacement and stale writers](../../knowledge/state-and-recovery.md#replacement-and-stale-writers) requires an enforceable retirement boundary, non-reused generations/revisions or another verified exclusion mechanism, failure semantics and reconciliation. |
| K03 | P2 | A custom error summary in a submit handler could be bypassed by native interactive validation | [Validation ownership](../../knowledge/forms-and-navigation.md#validation-and-error-recovery) requires a coherent native/custom feedback and focus path across button, implicit and scripted entry points; it does not mandate novalidate universally. |
| K04 | P1 | A new key and review could bypass an unresolved earlier action, allowing both effects | [Dispatch and changed intent](../../knowledge/uncertain-outcomes.md#dispatch-and-changed-intent) preserves immutable submitted intent separately, retains the old reference and gates overlapping new consequences on reconciliation or an explicit supported policy. |
| K05 | P2 | Reload recovery was promised without a pre-dispatch recovery boundary | The [dispatch contract](../../knowledge/uncertain-outcomes.md#dispatch-and-changed-intent) requires acknowledged recovery identity or documented independent service discovery, including retention/mapping failures and truthful reduced-capability limits. |
| K06 | P2 | Observation could spin indefinitely or poll without a pacing/freshness decision | The [service worksheet](../../knowledge/uncertain-outcomes.md#service-contract-worksheet) and [recovery policy](../../knowledge/uncertain-outcomes.md#safe-retry-and-recovery) require wait thresholds, observation pacing, stale feedback and supported escalation. Elapsed time supplies no outcome or retry permission. |

K01 was independently reported by the state and forms reviewers and is counted once. No material factual error was identified in the initial HTTP, storage or accessibility summaries during these targeted reviews. The defects were missing connections between otherwise useful rules, rather than proof that the underlying APIs or source guidance were incorrect.

## Revision and evaluation boundary

Chapter generation inputs were frozen at 0.2-draft after these corrections. Each fresh generator receives only its brief and selected library content, without these findings, earlier generated project designs or root review answers. The library itself contains earlier trial counterexamples, so the consumer/storefront exercise remains a rehearsal. Current hashes and immutable first-pass artifacts are in [evidence.json](evidence.json); the unchanged historical [P0 evidence](../../references/p0-evidence.json) records the initial draft snapshot rather than the revised files' current hashes.

No skill, template, theme, catalog, preview, tool or workflow implementation is changed by this review. Browser/storage/network/assistive-technology tests and real service contracts remain separate work.
