# Project examples

The portfolio and agent operations examples are self-contained project specifications derived from Editorial:

- [Portfolio](portfolio/design.md) preserves expressive typography and comfortable reading layouts.
- [Agent operations dashboard](agent-operations-dashboard/design.md) adapts density, geometry, motion, and interaction flow.

Both are drafts for hypothetical requirements. They do not establish that a UI, backend, approval system, or cancellation guarantee has been implemented.

The [Editorial preview](../themes/editorial/preview/README.md) demonstrates representative layouts and local simulation behavior. It does not implement all the requirements of either project example.

Use an example to understand adaptation decisions, not as a universal project requirement. Supply real project content, supported capabilities, language requirements, and evidence before implementation. Retain the example's source credits and update the adaptation record when changing its rules.

The reusable Editorial theme lives in [themes/editorial](../themes/editorial/README.md).

The [workflow evaluation](workflow-evaluation/README.md) separately records a fresh bilingual journal brief and its generated design document. It is evidence about the preparation workflow, not an additional theme.

The [nine-theme coverage assessment](coverage-evaluation/README.md) compares the original five project families and a separate consumer-app case, then records fresh Layered consumer and Gallery storefront design trials. Their briefs, first-generation reading logs, review history and source credits are retained. They are unimplemented project drafts with explicit persistence/service/asset gates. The [skills roadmap](coverage-evaluation/skills-roadmap.md) is a proposal; no skill or shared knowledge chapter was implemented by that evaluation.

The [P0 review and design trials](p0-evaluation/README.md) follow the knowledge research with three fresh generated documents, immutable first drafts, independent findings and one correction pass. Reading logs and capability gates remain explicit; no skill, UI or service is implemented by this evaluation.

The [updated preparation and opt-in review trials](skill-evaluation/README.md) exercise the implemented workflow definitions on the three known briefs and a fresh operations brief, with explicit review, scope probes, retained first drafts and measured source reads. Their records separate document decisions from unperformed product/runtime/usability checks.
