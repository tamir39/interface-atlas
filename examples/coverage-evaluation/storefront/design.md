# Form & Field design

Status: reviewed draft specification; no UI built or usability validation performed. Review corrections are recorded in evaluation.md; the first draft is preserved separately. Theme: Gallery `0.1.0-draft`, reviewed 2026-10-15; Atlas record `atlas-gallery-research-2026-10-15`. Local source snapshot: Interface Atlas `782159e8e2510933c76989ff9fd9546d282c62cd`.

## Project requirements and evidence boundary

Form & Field is a fictional independent homeware studio with roughly 50 products, an English guest storefront, and USD display. Visitors discover objects, inspect accurate imagery, select available variants/quantities, maintain a cart draft, review authoritative commercial information, and enter hosted checkout. Backend contracts, provider, reservation rules, catalog, media, rights, brand assets, font files, and stack are absent.

**Atlas inheritance** below means Gallery's proposed visual/media/accessibility rules; it does not mean proven commerce support. **Project proposal** means decisions drafted for this brief without additional research or validation. **Contract gate** means implementation depends on a backend/product decision. Product interaction sections are project proposals throughout.

Gallery is selected for unboxed imagery, quiet sans typography, complete-object inspection, and explicit media recovery. Its README describes storefront suitability as conditional and supplies no cart/checkout model. Botanical was shortlisted for calm lifestyle identity but introduces organic geometry and serif emphasis; Editorial favors reading-led boutique stories. Neither alternative solves the missing commerce contracts. Retain Gallery's comfortable density, flat surfaces, near-square controls, and still images; adapt its fixed inline collection viewer into collection and product pages.

## Typography, spacing, and geometry

Inherit Inter, designed by Rasmus Andersson, normal weights 400/500/600. Until a licensed package is supplied, use `Inter, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif` with local fallback; naming Inter does not prove it rendered. English punctuation, currency, long product names, weight availability, and fallback wrapping require checks. All text remains selectable.

Project role selection: page H1 `clamp(2.5rem,5vw,4.5rem)/1.1`, weight 400, tracking `-0.035em`; section H2 `clamp(1.75rem,3vw,3rem)/1.2`, 400; product-card headings `1.25rem/1.35`, 500; body and price `1rem/1.65`, 400; labels/captions `0.875rem/1.5`, 500; inputs `1rem/1.5`. Cart prices/totals use tabular numerals as a project adaptation. Currency display is explicitly USD with two decimals; arithmetic/rounding comes from the contract, never floating-point client inference.

Retain spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px, 8px label gaps, 12px related-control gaps, and 48–96px sections. Content maximum 1280px; gutters 16px below 768px, 32px above. Media corners 0; panels/controls 4px; no elevation. Targets minimum proposed 44×44px, including quantity controls and image selectors. Do not shrink target or body size for cart density.

## Pages and responsive behavior

Project routes are proposals, not working URLs: collection `/shop`, stable product `/products/{slug}`, and `/cart`; a checkout return/status destination depends on contract. Header order: text wordmark, Shop, Search, Cart with unit count; appearance controls live in the footer/settings region. No account, wishlist, loyalty, or agent navigation. Use real anchors and preserve modified-click behavior.

Phone baseline, tested eventually from 320px: single-column content, product media before purchasing controls, cart lines followed by totals. Collection uses one column below 480px, two at 480px, three at 900px; breakpoints are content-fit proposals. At 900px product detail becomes a 7/5 media/purchase split and cart a 2/1 lines/summary split. DOM order follows reading order. No masonry, horizontal page scrolling, sticky purchase overlay, or hidden purchase information. At enlarged text or insufficient width, stack again.

Collection contains H1, brief studio introduction, labeled search, category filter, result count, product grid, and explicit empty/error/loading regions. Proposed search matches supplied title/category/material; filter fields require catalog confirmation and unavailable data is not invented. For this small catalog, prefer filtering one fully delivered catalog snapshot; if delivery is paginated, search/filter authority and completeness must be resolved before implementation. Product cards show complete-object image, name, category, and clearly scoped price/availability observation. Use “View product” anchors; variants require detail inspection rather than quick-add assumptions.

Product page order: breadcrumb/back-to-results, H1, media group, description/material/dimensions when supplied, variant selection, current commercial information, quantity, Add to cart, and inline feedback. Preserve collection query/filter/scroll context in navigation. Cart lists product/variant, image, observed unit price, quantity, line state, Remove, quote summary, and checkout action. Loading/error pages retain navigation and retry; never present an empty cart merely because loading failed.

## Media and asset delivery

Inherit native `img`/`picture`, intrinsic dimensions and reserved space, useful context-dependent alt text, original image colors in all appearances, and off-image captions/credits. Product/variant photography must accurately represent the selected item. Use complete-object `contain` in a proposed stable 4:5 card frame with neutral page field; detail uses natural ratio or contain without cropping. Contextual brand imagery may use deliberate cover only with focal-point/crop records and an uncropped product view. Never stretch images.

Provide ordinary labeled thumbnail buttons, e.g. “Show side view, 2 of 4,” with pressed/current indication; the selected image remains still. A larger inline full-image view preserves aspect ratio and keyboard access without an unresearched zoom/modal interaction. Failed images retain title, description, caption, and “Image unavailable” plus per-image Retry where supported. Pending/retry uses actual request events; stale image completions cannot replace current variant metadata. Missing imagery uses a clearly labeled neutral placeholder, never fabricated stock photography.

Gate actual delivery on catalog/media IDs, variant-image mapping, intrinsic dimensions, descriptive alternatives, photographer/creator, license/permission, credit wording, approved crops, formats, and derivative pipeline. Gallery's AI illustrations are not product photos and are not selected. Prioritize the first visible product image; lazy-load lower imagery. Propose responsive widths 320/640/960/1440px only where originals support them, with measured `sizes`; no unnecessary CSS-hidden duplicates. Asset weight/LCP/CLS budgets need real assets and device/network measurement.

## Components, states, and recovery

Inherit visible labels, necessary boundaries, immediate focus, and text-based state feedback. Project-specific controls use native search/select, fieldsets with legends and radio choices for color/size, and labeled quantity number input with increment/decrement buttons. Color names accompany swatches. No arbitrary default variant: require a complete valid selection; a single valid combination may be explicitly preselected and named. Unavailable choices remain discoverable with “Unavailable”; combination rules need backend/catalog evidence. Quantity is an integer starting at 1; minimum/maximum/step rules beyond that are gated. Invalid entries receive associated inline errors and remain correctable.

Hover uses defined pairs; keyboard focus is a 3px ink outline offset 5px, unclipped; forced-colors uses Highlight. Selected controls have ink markers. Pending actions retain contextual labels and expose busy state. Empty search offers Clear filters, returning focus to Search; no-results differs from retrieval failure. Retry preserves filters/selection. Add to cart reports a local draft change, never stock reservation. Cart edits announce item and quantity. Remove keeps one local Undo slot, available only during this cart-page visit until the next successful cart mutation, navigation away, reload, or checkout creation begins; no timed expiry is proposed. Explain that lifetime beside Undo. Repeated removals replace the slot with the latest line and announce that the previous removal is no longer undoable. Any add, quantity/variant edit, or accepted cross-tab replacement clears it. Re-adding the same variant is a new addition that invalidates the old slot and merges only the explicitly added quantity into any matching current line; it cannot also resurrect the removed quantity. Undo restores purchase intent and requires reconciliation, never stock or a guaranteed price. Reload or checkout cancellation does not restore expired undo history.

Disabled checkout always has adjacent explanation. Network errors remain inline and recoverable; essential messages never rely on vanishing toasts. Keep keyboard focus on an edited control; after removal move to the next line heading or cart heading and politely announce the latest removal and available Undo. Name Undo with that product/variant; after activation focus the restored line heading, retaining any unresolved availability error. Product page navigation focuses its H1 after route completion. Image choices retain focus and announce selection politely.

## Cart ownership, persistence, and reconciliation

**Project proposal:** the browser owns purchase intent only: product ID, stable variant ID, integer quantity, local revision, and update time. It does not own prices, stock, quote totals, reservation, payment, or order success. The backend owns those commercial facts. A displayed catalog observation is explicitly time-scoped and never a reservation. Do not persist price/stock as authority.

Use a proposed versioned `form-field.cart.v1` local-storage record and separate appearance preference. Validate schema, types, quantities, bounds against malicious/invalid data, and handle parse/read/write/quota failures. Restore a valid draft on same-browser reload, immediately mark it “Checking price and availability,” and request reconciliation. If storage fails, keep the draft in memory and state “This cart may not survive reload.” Do not promise persistence. A malformed record is quarantined in place: leave its stored bytes untouched, suspend all automatic writes to that key, and start an explicitly temporary empty in-memory draft. Explain that the previous cart could not be read. Ordinary edits cannot erase or replace it. Only an informed Reset saved cart action, after explaining that the unreadable saved record will be discarded and the temporary draft saved, may overwrite it; failure retains the guarded temporary state. No order is inferred and no personal/payment data is stored.

Persist successful local edits immediately only when storage works and no malformed-record quarantine is active. Debounce quote refresh if useful but invalidate the prior quote immediately; use local revision/request generation to discard late responses. Quotes belong to an exact cart revision. Editing quantity/variant/removing a line makes checkout unavailable until reconciliation. Another-tab storage change triggers an explicit review/reload choice rather than silently overwriting active intent; conflict resolution is a project proposal requiring testing.

Reconcile on restore, relevant cart edits, reentry after a stale observation, and immediately before checkout. Exact freshness policy is a contract gate. Preserve unavailable/removed/unknown-ID lines with named issues and repair/remove actions. Do not silently substitute variants, lower quantity, or accept increased prices. Show old observation versus new authoritative value where both are known; require explicit review before continuing. A failed quote retains the draft and labels prior values stale. It cannot unlock checkout.

## Backend handoff and checkout recovery gates

These are proposed capabilities and response semantics, not endpoint names or existing services. Before implementation agree: stable catalog/variant identities; permitted combinations and quantity limits; catalog freshness; quote request/response schema; currency and precise money representation; line-level failures; total components and scope; tax/shipping determination; quote validity; inventory/reservation policy; authentication/session needs for guests; and error/retry semantics. Never show invented zero tax/shipping or call a partial subtotal “Total.” Present known subtotal plus unresolved charges until an authoritative complete amount or explicitly scoped handoff quote exists.

Checkout additionally requires a backend-created hosted session bound to the reviewed cart/quote revision; allowed redirect origin; safe return/cancel URLs; idempotency/deduplication rules; safe durable attempt reference; retrievable authoritative session/order status; expiry/cancellation/retry policy; and provider selection. The supplied recovery capability must locate and reconcile an attempt even if the creation response is lost and cart storage is unavailable; a reference held only in losable browser memory or that cart key is insufficient. Agree an independent, durable recovery route and its guest access/privacy rules before creating any session. If safe recovery is unavailable, block session creation with a truthful explanation; a temporary cart does not establish checkout recoverability. No reservation timer is shown without a supplied contract. Checkout implementation remains gated until these are resolved.

Proposed flow: validate current selection → update draft → reconcile → review material changes and authoritative scoped totals → create hosted handoff → navigate only to validated returned destination. Pending creation prevents duplicate activation. Network timeout after creation is an **unknown outcome**, not failure or success. Preserve cart and any safe opaque attempt reference; show “Checkout status is unknown. Check status before trying again.” Use the contracted independent recovery route when the local reference/cart cannot be recovered. Status retrieval and recovery across reload must be contracted before creation; never persist a secret checkout URL/token casually. Without these capabilities, offer a truthful blocked state and manual recovery information supplied by the merchant; do not blindly recreate a session.

On return, URL labels or client history cannot establish payment/order success. Ask the backend for authoritative status: pending/unknown keeps recovery available; cancelled/expired restores editable draft only when safe under the contract; confirmed completion can offer a receipt/reference only if returned by the backend. Clear only purchased matching quantities/lines after confirmed completion; preserve later cart edits. Distinguish checkout session creation, payment, and accepted order according to supplied status semantics. No success screen or fabricated order is specified as working.

## Accessibility, appearance, and motion

Inherit native landmarks, continuous headings, skip link, logical tab order, labels, alt policy, 44px target proposal, and opaque focus/text surfaces. Announce search counts and cart/quote changes concisely; avoid announcing every keystroke or stealing focus for background responses. Associate errors with fields, and provide a focusable error summary after failed explicit submission. Keyboard-only users can select variants, inspect all media, edit/remove/undo cart lines, retry, review changes, and enter hosted checkout; hosted accessibility remains a provider contract and validation task.

Light/Dark/System uses System by default, honors live OS changes only while System is selected, and applies valid saved preference early to limit flash; unavailable preference storage retains usable defaults. Media is never inverted, dimmed, or recolored. Gallery's optional color/background/border transitions use 180ms `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)` only when OS motion permits; reduced motion makes them immediate. No entrance, image motion, autoplay, animated scroll, or decorative effects are selected. Business state never waits for animation. A no-JavaScript failure needs readable navigation/content and a truthful explanation of unavailable dynamic cart capabilities; fallback scope depends on the eventual stack.
