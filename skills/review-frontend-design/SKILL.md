# Review frontend design

Review the requested artifact/interface against its brief and governing rules. This opt-in workflow is not required for ordinary edits or preparation and does not authorize implementation, theme replacement or project-file changes. Locate Atlas through the links below; keep it distinct from the consuming project.

## Choose the evidence and scope

Start with the brief, project rules and supplied evidence. A source/rendered review needs no design.md. Distinguish inferred intent from requirements and proposed behavior from observations. Inspect the relevant source/page for the requested review mode; record what was exercised. Unavailable tools/services limit coverage rather than establish a pass.

Use resolved project rules first. For a remaining review question, read the known section directly or use [the knowledge index](../../knowledge/README.md) to locate it. Follow only necessary dependencies, then stop; reuse available conclusions/read records without repeating indexes or whole chapters. Check theme/style inheritance, overrides and fallbacks only when implicated. A preference or shared-profile pass alone does not establish compatibility.

Inspect selected pattern rules only for a relevant declared pattern or unresolved behavior; use [discovery](../../patterns/README.md) if unidentified. Existing decisions skip catalog browsing. Compare actual states/evidence; previews are not universal requirements.

## Trace tasks and test findings

Trace essential paths and applicable alternate states. Assess information order, hierarchy, labels, readable type, semantic color and responsive/input behavior in those tasks. Select accessibility, state/forms, remote-outcome or Agent Experience guidance only for an actual review question; do not add unrelated capabilities.

For persistent edits, remote effects or changing agent proposals, select applicable traces from the [state-transition profile](references/state-transitions.md); do not read every linked chapter. A future-test list does not establish a completed behavior contract.

For each candidate, identify its requirement or supported usability concern, precise location/state, trigger and task consequence. Check nearby rules and alternative paths for counterevidence. Distinguish standards, project contracts, heuristics and taste; a legitimate theme choice is not a defect because another skill prefers it differently.

Apply relevant craft guidance such as better-ui within user/project/design/accessibility rules. Fixed polish preferences alone do not establish defects or require a companion-family sweep or second report. In implicated narrow layouts, inspect fixed/sticky controls against content and feedback; passing overflow assertions does not rule out occlusion.

Prioritize task impact/recoverability and consolidate duplicate symptoms. An owned external dependency can be an open contract; flag unsupported promises or ungated dependent actions. Keep unknown service behavior unknown. Synthetic users, scores and source inspection do not prove usability, accessibility conformance or execution.

## Deliver the review

Report actionable findings first with severity/reason, location, trigger/consequence, evidence and bounded correction. Name unresolved inputs/owners when known. If none, say so and describe checks and limits.

Include concise coverage: material read, task/state paths, applicable appearance/viewport/input contexts and unperformed checks. Separate document, source, browser, keyboard, assistive-technology and service evidence. Summarize raw results rather than dumping nested evidence. Suggest fixes without applying them; do not launch preparation or another audit.

Preserve credits and distinguish Atlas synthesis from upstream material. Use selected guidance's concise credits. Open [adaptation records](../../references/skill-adaptations.md), research ledgers or full terms only for an attribution/permission question or copying/distributing a covered artifact or substantial adaptation; carry required notices. Use existing read records or metadata hashes rather than rereading content for fingerprints.
