Gallery is an original Interface Atlas synthesis by Tamir / Phi Vuong Tuong Tam with Codex AI assistance. Its composition, proposed palette and geometry, native code, fictional collection, and study descriptions were created for this preview.

The three raster studies are AI-generated illustrative assets created for Atlas. They are not photographs of real products, buildings, or a named photographer’s work. The original colors are retained in both appearances.

Shopify’s theme design guidance informs the image-resilience and content-hierarchy research. Inter is designed by Rasmus Andersson and contributors; no font binaries are included or downloaded. Web-platform and W3C references inform image loading and native interaction semantics. No third-party markup, code, or imagery is imported.
