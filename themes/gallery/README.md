# Gallery

Imagery determines the composition. Quiet sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, unboxed media, small off-image captions, flat surfaces, and near-square controls let a collection establish its own character. A restrained neutral interface supports both complete-object inspection and deliberate contextual crops.

Gallery is an **original Interface Atlas synthesis for Tamir (Phi Vuong Tuong Tam), with Codex AI assistance**. Shopify's design documentation and Dawn/Horizon listings inform image-led presentation and content resilience; web.dev, W3C WAI, and web standards inform image delivery and interaction. No referenced theme implementation is adapted or imported. Every numerical value, layout, crop policy, artwork, and workflow is an Atlas proposal.

This direction differs from Editorial's reading-led serif composition, Swiss's structural rules, and Botanical's organic geometry. Gallery makes the media system itself a first-class design decision: what can be cropped, what must remain complete, how captions behave, and what survives unavailable imagery.

## Suitability

These are Atlas assessments, not measured usability, conversion, or performance results.

| Project type | Fit | Reason and adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Image portfolio | Strong | Work can lead the composition while names, roles, and evidence remain ordinary text. Record real image credits and permissions. |
| Collection or exhibition site | Strong | Consistent captions and deliberate ratios connect varied pieces without flattening their differences. Add indexing appropriate to the collection. |
| Design catalog or lookbook | Strong | Large media and quiet metadata support visual comparison. Keep object accuracy separate from contextual crops. |
| Architecture or interiors site | Strong | Wide views and detail images can form a coherent account of space. Each image needs its own creator and rights record. |
| Boutique storefront | Conditional | The visual direction suits image-heavy discovery. Variants, prices, stock, cart, and checkout need researched project flows; they are not supplied by this theme. |
| Publication | Conditional | Use image-led essays and captions, then add the reading, navigation, and media-credit conventions needed for long content. |
| Agent workspace or dense analytics | Strained | Large media and comfortable presentation provide little advantage for frequent operational tasks. Additional data, density, authority, and recovery rules are required. |

A theme supplies visual language rather than a complete product model. A project records its selected adaptation and relevant [Agent Experience decisions](../../knowledge/agent-experience.md) when those apply.

## Preview and validation

The [live Gallery reference preview](https://tamir39.github.io/interface-atlas/themes/gallery/preview/) demonstrates an original fictional collection, a local inline viewer, content filtering, media recovery, and neutral component specimens. [Preview notes](preview/README.md) define its scope. [Index.html](preview/index.html) also opens locally without a build or server; its three local image assets must remain beside it.

The photography-style studies are **AI-generated illustrations**, not photographs of real products, actual architecture, or a named photographer's work. Their prompts, dimensions, and hashes are recorded in the [asset provenance record](preview/assets/README.md). The preview includes no remote imagery, font files, external scripts, or source-theme code.

Read [validation.md](validation.md) for the browser evidence actually collected. Opaque token calculations remain in [design.md](design.md). This entry stays **draft**: a bounded preview does not validate real catalog imagery, every adaptation, intended fonts, assistive technology, complete accessibility, real network conditions, or commercial behavior.
