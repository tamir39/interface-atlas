Brand identity / Fictional case study

A little closer
to your neighbors.

Give a neighborhood swap club a warm, useful identity that feels welcoming before the first hello.

Inside the Sprout project +

The starting point

A local club needs a name, a signup card, and a simple way to explain what members can share.

The design decisions

A growing two-leaf symbol introduces the idea of exchange. Large words make the invitation direct; a mint surface keeps the tone light. The signup card uses plain labels and a visible next step.

This is an original illustrative brief. No client, launch, or measured outcome is claimed.

A sketchbook for half-formed thoughts. Make it easy to collect an idea without deciding what it becomes.

Inside the Daydream project +

The starting point

A creative notebook needs quick capture and a calm way to revisit older ideas without ranking every note.

The design decisions

The writing surface stays quiet while labels and controls carry a little personality. Notes remain selectable, the add action has an explicit label, and a native disclosure keeps supporting context close.

This is an original illustrative brief. No app, user research result, or shipped product is claimed.

Campaign / Fictional case study

Make something.
Find your people.

A community making afternoon needs a bright invitation and an easy path from curiosity to joining in.

Inside the Come Around project +

The starting point

A free making afternoon needs a poster and a short event page that tells newcomers what to expect.

The design decisions

Overlapping circles suggest people gathering. The invitation leads with the activity, then gives the time, place, and access information in a readable order. The join action remains visible at small sizes.

This is an original illustrative brief. No event, attendance, or campaign outcome is claimed.
