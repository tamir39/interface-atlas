# Layered — optional effects

Revision: **0.1.0-draft**. Reviewed: **2026-10-15**.

Layered is complete with opaque tonal surfaces, coherent geometry, readable content and immediate state changes. Every effect and numerical treatment below is an **Atlas proposal**. No Material or Fluent effect implementation, shape library, icon set, shader, renderer or third-party artwork is imported.

## Tonal composition

Assign a surface a useful role before adding another container. A Low navigation plane, Surface working region, Secondary supporting panel and Raised editor can establish context without multiple shadow ramps. Selection uses Tint, readable Ink and an explicit current marker; it remains recognizable without interpreting the hue.

Keep working surfaces opaque in both appearances. Quiet static geometric artwork may echo the same rounded vocabulary in a header or empty state, but it must remain decorative and must not carry a project's name, task status, count or action. Code-native shapes are original Atlas artwork rather than copied Material shapes or logos. Artwork cannot hide useful content during initialization.

Repeated **24px panels**, **12px controls**, **999px navigation pills** and a **28px modal** form the proposed geometry. Do not add an unrelated radius to every card. Avoid nested card shells when a heading and space already communicate grouping. Preserve meaningful reading order when arranging a decorative composition beside content.

## Selective elevation

An optional original modal shadow is `0 24px 64px rgba(0,0,0,.24)`. It suggests the temporary editor sits above the workspace. The native modal's opaque Raised fill, visible boundaries, accessible name and focus behavior still work without that shadow. Ordinary project cards do not need a resting shadow.

A translucent backdrop can dim the blocked workspace while the editor is open. This is separation for a modal task, not a translucent content material. Backdrop and shadow are composed visuals outside the opaque color arithmetic; inspect actual light/dark combinations and forced-colors fallback. Never put essential text on the dimmed background and treat its contrast as an active dialog control.

There is no required glass, backdrop blur, Mica, acrylic, neumorphic inset shading, glow, grain, photographic texture, parallax, cursor follower or persistent effect. The fictional workspace neither samples a desktop wallpaper nor reproduces a native operating-system material.

## States and motion

Use a clear boundary for controls and a visible focus outline independent of optional background feedback. A tonal selection and a focus indicator can appear together. Priority labels remain text; decorative artwork and colors do not establish permissions, safety, completion or authority.

Only optional **160ms color/background/border feedback** accompanies ordinary control interaction when motion is allowed. Focus, selection, filtering, checklist counts, form validation and modal closure are immediate. No ripple, scale, entrance, fade or moving illustration is part of the reference. Follow [motion.md](motion.md).

Material Design and Fluent are credited conceptual comparisons in [sources.md](sources.md). Their software licenses do not clear copied website assets or brand identity. The [validation record](validation.md) establishes only the representative browser checks actually performed, not every composited effect, real-device performance or complete accessibility.
